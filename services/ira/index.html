Open a Precious Metals IRA

Understand the questions involved in evaluating a self-directed precious metals IRA. This page is educational and does not open an account or provide tax, legal, or investment advice.

What to understand first

  • Not all coins and bullion products qualify for IRA ownership.
  • A self-directed IRA generally involves an eligible custodian and approved storage arrangements.
  • Custodial, setup, transaction, storage, and insurance fees may apply.
  • Distributions, transfers, tax treatment, and eligibility depend on individual circumstances and current rules.
  • Precious metals can fluctuate in value and are not guaranteed to produce a return.

Speak with an independent tax or financial professional and verify all providers, fees, and requirements before proceeding.
